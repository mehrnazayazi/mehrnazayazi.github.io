Miscellaneous

Outside of work

Outside of research, I enjoy spending most of my free time outdoors, making the most of California's beautiful landscapes. I love hiking, rock climbing, and camping, but I'm always open to trying any outdoor activity. Photography, especially capturing people and landscapes, is another hobby of mine. I also enjoy running and swimming, watching philosophy videos on YouTube, exploring French New Wave films, and learning new languages, both natural and programming. During winter, when outdoor activities are less accessible, I enjoy attending performances and plays in Southern California. One of my favorite experiences was watching the first synopsis of the ballet Woolf Works last February.

When indoors, I enjoy writing, often just for myself. I’ve also written articles for the Computer Engineering Department magazine at Amirkabir University of Technology during my undergraduate years. As an aspiring chef, I love experimenting with new recipes inspired by traditional Persian dishes—pennyroyal is my favorite ingredient to work with.
